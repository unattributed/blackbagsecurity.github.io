  <section class="m-section" aria-labelledby="lifecycle-title">
    <div class="marketing-wrap">
      <div class="m-section__intro">
        <p class="m-eyebrow">Engagement lifecycle</p>
        <h2 id="lifecycle-title">One connected chain of <span class="m-accent">authority and evidence.</span></h2>
        <p>Each phase creates the conditions for the next. The engagement should remain explainable from the original authorization through the final retest.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="m-process" aria-label="Six phase engagement lifecycle">
        <article><strong>Authorize</strong><span>Objectives, scope, rules, safety, access, escalation</span></article>
        <article><strong>Test</strong><span>Map, reason, execute, validate, stop when conditions require it</span></article>
        <article><strong>Evidence</strong><span>Capture enough context to support reproduction and review</span></article>
        <article><strong>Report</strong><span>Separate facts, impact, uncertainty, and recommendations</span></article>
        <article><strong>Remediate</strong><span>Help translate evidence into practical corrective action</span></article>
        <article><strong>Retest</strong><span>Replay the original failure condition and verify closure</span></article>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="m-section m-section--soft" id="authority" aria-labelledby="authority-title">
    <div class="marketing-wrap m-split">
      <div class="m-copy">
        <p class="m-eyebrow">Authority before capability</p>
        <h2 id="authority-title">Know exactly what is allowed <span class="m-accent">before execution begins.</span></h2>
        <p>Authorization is an engineering input. The target, environment, credentials, timing, prohibited actions, production constraints, safety conditions, escalation path, and stop conditions must be clear enough to guide real decisions during testing.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="m-grid m-grid--2">
        <article class="m-card"><h3>Objectives</h3><p>The decision the work must support and the questions that need evidence.</p></article>
        <article class="m-card"><h3>Boundaries</h3><p>Exact targets, environments, identities, permitted actions, and exclusions.</p></article>
        <article class="m-card"><h3>Safety</h3><p>Production constraints, protected systems, stop conditions, and escalation.</p></article>
        <article class="m-card"><h3>Change control</h3><p>Material scope or authority changes are explicit rather than inferred mid-engagement.</p></article>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="m-section" id="evidence" aria-labelledby="evidence-title">
    <div class="marketing-wrap m-split">
      <div class="m-copy">
        <p class="m-eyebrow">Evidence first</p>
        <h2 id="evidence-title">A signal is not <span class="m-accent">a finding.</span></h2>
        <p>Tool output, scanner alerts, unexpected responses, and suspicious behavior are leads. A finding requires enough context to establish the condition, impact, affected path, limitations, and confidence behind the conclusion.</p>
      </div>
      <blockquote class="m-quote">The report should say what the evidence proves, what it does not prove, and what still needs to be established.</blockquote>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="m-section m-section--soft" id="client-protection" aria-labelledby="protection-title">
    <div class="marketing-wrap">
      <div class="m-section__intro">
        <p class="m-eyebrow">Client protection</p>
        <h2 id="protection-title">The engagement itself is <span class="m-accent">a sensitive system.</span></h2>
        <p>Credentials, architecture, findings, evidence, logs, screenshots, client communications, and working state can be as sensitive as the systems being assessed. Handling requirements therefore belong inside the delivery method, not in an afterthought.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="m-grid m-grid--4">
        <article class="m-card"><span class="m-card__label">Collect</span><h3>Only what is useful</h3><p>Avoid unnecessary client data and reduce exposure created by the engagement itself.</p></article>
        <article class="m-card"><span class="m-card__label">Protect</span><h3>Keep context bounded</h3><p>Use engagement-specific workspaces, access controls, secure channels, and deliberate storage.</p></article>
        <article class="m-card"><span class="m-card__label">Sanitize</span><h3>Remove what does not belong</h3><p>Reports and evidence packages should not carry avoidable secrets or unrelated sensitive material.</p></article>
        <article class="m-card"><span class="m-card__label">Retain</span><h3>Keep only what is justified</h3><p>Retention should support delivery and retest without becoming indefinite accumulation.</p></article>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="m-section" id="visibility" aria-labelledby="visibility-title">
    <div class="marketing-wrap m-split">
      <div class="m-copy">
        <p class="m-eyebrow">Client visibility</p>
        <h2 id="visibility-title">No disappearing into <span class="m-accent">a black box.</span></h2>
        <p>Material blockers, scope questions, risk discoveries, access problems, schedule effects, and meaningful findings should surface while there is still time to act. Communication is part of delivery quality.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="m-grid m-grid--2">
        <article class="m-card"><h3>Known status</h3><p>Clients can understand what has been completed, what remains, and what is blocked.</p></article>
        <article class="m-card"><h3>Known limitations</h3><p>Coverage gaps and access constraints are stated explicitly instead of being hidden by a polished report.</p></article>
        <article class="m-card"><h3>Early escalation</h3><p>Conditions that threaten safety, coverage, evidence quality, or schedule are raised promptly.</p></article>
        <article class="m-card"><h3>Decision-ready reporting</h3><p>Technical depth is preserved while the consequence and required action remain clear.</p></article>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="m-section m-section--soft" id="closure" aria-labelledby="closure-title">
    <div class="marketing-wrap m-split">
      <div class="m-copy">
        <p class="m-eyebrow">Verified closure</p>
        <h2 id="closure-title">A fix is not proven until <span class="m-accent">the failure path changes.</span></h2>
        <p>Retest starts from the original evidence. The relevant condition is replayed under current authorization, the changed behavior is recorded, and any remaining limitation is documented.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="m-panel">
        <div class="m-panel__head">Closure standard</div>
        <div class="m-panel__body"><ul class="m-checks"><li><div><strong>Original condition known</strong><span>Retest is anchored to the finding that was actually established.</span></div></li><li><div><strong>Remediation state known</strong><span>The change being validated is identified.</span></div></li><li><div><strong>Failure path replayed</strong><span>The relevant technical path is exercised again.</span></div></li><li><div><strong>Result recorded</strong><span>Closed, partially remediated, still present, or not testable is stated clearly.</span></div></li></ul></div>
